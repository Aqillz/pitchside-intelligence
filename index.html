    Methodology and data

    Explore what the numbers measure or replay your own synthetic events.

    Expected goals and expected threat

    xG estimates a shot’s chance value from distance and pressure. xT compares the threat at the start and end of a pass or shot. Team totals and momentum use positive xT gains. Both models are illustrative heuristics.

    Zone 14 and report generation

    Zone 14 is the central pocket just outside each penalty area. An entry counts when a pass starts outside that pocket and ends inside it. The report combines aggregates with preset tactical rules.

    Privacy and synthetic data

    Teams, shirt numbers and match events in the demo are fictional. The dashboard uses no API keys, accounts, analytics scripts or browser storage. Imported events stay in the current tab.
